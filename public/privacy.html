Privacy Policy

Last updated October 15, 2026

This policy explains what BiteBeacon ("we", "us") collects when you use the BiteBeacon website and app, why, and the choices you have. We collect as little as we can, we do not sell your information, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Without an account (demo mode)

You can try BiteBeacon without signing up. In demo mode your allergy selections, household, pantry and scan history stay in your browser's memory and disappear when you close the tab. Nothing about you is saved on our servers; a lookup only adds one to the anonymous scan total described below.

What we store when you create an account

  • Account details: your email address and a securely hashed password, handled by our sign-in provider, Supabase.
  • Allergy profile: the allergens you select, and the date you agreed to store them.
  • Household: names you give to other people in your household (up to eight) and the allergens you select for each. This can include information about children. Only add people you are allowed to share information about, and use first names or nicknames if you prefer.
  • Language: the app language you choose.
  • Pantry: products you choose to save, including a copy of the product details at the time, the expiry date you enter, and whether you marked it used or thrown out.

Allergy information is health-related information. We store it only to run the features you use, and only you can read it: the database blocks every account from seeing another account's rows.

What we do not store

  • Your general scan history. It stays on your device for the current session only.
  • What you scan. We keep one running total of how many barcode lookups have been made across the whole app, with or without an account. Each lookup adds one to that number; the barcode, the product and who looked it up are not recorded with it.
  • Camera images. The barcode scanner reads frames on your device; they are not uploaded or saved.
  • Your location, contacts or advertising identifiers.

Who else receives information

  • Food databases: when you look up a product, the barcode number is sent from your device to USDA FoodData Central and, if it is not found there, to Open Food Facts. Like any website, they can see your IP address. Your allergies and account details are never sent to them.
  • Supabase stores account data and sends sign-up and account emails on our behalf.
  • Netlify hosts the website and, like any web host, keeps short-term technical logs such as IP addresses.

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We may disclose information if required by law.

Storage in your browser

If you tick "Stay signed in", your sign-in session is kept in your browser's local storage so a reload keeps you signed in. Untick it on shared computers and the session ends when you close the tab. We also remember that choice in your browser. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your account data until you delete it. You can delete your account at any time in App details › Delete my account. That permanently removes your sign-in account, allergy profile, household and pantry from our database. Copies in our providers' backups expire on their own schedule.

You can view and change your allergies, household and pantry in the app at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, such as asking for a copy of your data. Contact us to use them.

Children

BiteBeacon accounts are for adults. We do not knowingly let children under 13 create accounts. A parent or guardian may add a child's name and allergies to their own household.

Security

Connections are encrypted with HTTPS, passwords are hashed, and access to saved data is limited to its owner. No system is perfectly secure, so use a password you do not use anywhere else.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and for significant changes we will tell you in the app before they apply.
