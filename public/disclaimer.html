Health disclaimer

Last updated October 15, 2026

BiteBeacon is an information aid. It can be wrong or incomplete, and it never tells you that a food is safe to eat.

Always read the ingredient list and allergen statement on the physical package before eating, serving or buying food. If you or someone in your care has a severe allergy, follow the plan from your doctor.

In an emergency, use epinephrine if prescribed and call 911 or your local emergency number.

What BiteBeacon does

BiteBeacon looks up a product by its barcode in public food databases (USDA FoodData Central, then Open Food Facts) and compares the ingredient text and allergen tags it finds against the allergies you selected. The best result it can show is "No selected allergens found", which only means the check did not find a match in the data available. It is not a safety verdict.

Why results can be wrong

  • Database records are submitted by manufacturers or volunteers and can be missing, outdated or attached to the wrong barcode.
  • Recipes, suppliers and labels change. The package in your hand may differ from the record.
  • Cross-contact ("may contain", shared equipment) is often not reported at all.
  • The ingredient check uses a limited English word list. It can miss unusual names, other languages or hidden sources.
  • BiteBeacon covers 15 common allergens only: milk, eggs, peanuts, tree nuts, soy, wheat, fish, crustacean shellfish, sesame, gluten, mollusks, mustard, celery, sulfites and lupin. It does not check other allergies, intolerances or dietary needs. The gluten check only looks for gluten grains in the available data; it does not confirm a product is gluten-free or suitable for celiac disease.
  • Translations of the app were drafted by machine and may contain mistakes.
  • Pantry expiry dates are entered by you. Reminders appear only inside the app and may not appear at all.

Not medical advice

BiteBeacon is not a medical device and does not give medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Nothing in the app replaces the advice of a doctor, allergist or dietitian.

Your responsibility

You decide what you and the people in your care eat. By using BiteBeacon you agree that you will not rely on it as the only check, and that the Terms of Use apply, including their limits on liability.
